What counts as better

You are judging the search results, not writing an answer yourself. Both responses answer the same question. Pick the one that would serve a reader better — and say they are about the same whenever that is the honest answer. A forced choice between two responses you cannot separate is a coin flip, and it is recorded as though it were a judgement.

Some results will be newer than you expect. That is a working search engine, not an error — do not mark a result down for describing something you have not heard of.
