How a score gets made

Every step is in the public repository, and every intermediate result is in the export.

four steps

  1. The same queries, to every vendor

    queries across categories, sent to all APIs and normalised to the same top-10 depth. Without that, the most verbose vendor wins on volume.

  2. Three judges, three model families

    Each response is scored by one Anthropic, one OpenAI and one Google model against a published rubric. LLM judges prefer text that reads like their own; using one family would bake that preference into every number.

  3. Median, and only when complete

    The published score is the median of the three, and responses missing any judge are dropped rather than averaged over what is left. A partly-computed number in the same column as a complete one is worse than a missing one.

  4. Everything published, including the holes

    Individual judge scores, per-response latency and cost, and the coverage of every cell ship as CSV. You can recompute every number on this site from the export.

The scores cluster high

every scored query
Almost everything lands between 7 and 10, which suggests the query set is not hard enough to discriminate cleanly at the top. Harder queries are the next change to it.
Absolute scores are soft
Every vendor faces every judge, so the ranking survives the spread above. A claim like "scores 8.4 out of 10" does not: it would move on judge choice alone.
There is no human calibration yet
The methodology calls for a human-labelled gold set scored monthly against the ensemble. It has not been built. Until it is, the judges are unaudited, and this is the first thing a sharp reviewer should attack.
Two well-known vendors are missing
Tavily and Brave are deliberately excluded: their terms of service are broad enough to cover third-party benchmarking, and no written consent has been sought. Why the set is what it is.
One place, one moment
All queries were issued from a single location at a single time. Search results vary by region and by hour, and this measures neither.
